# Experiments with Member-Function Pointers in C++

These examples explore member-function-pointer representations on specific
compilers and ABIs, and show how to generate an ordinary function-pointer
thunk with a template.

The thunk is portable for the member-function forms it supports; extracting
or reinterpreting a member-function pointer's representation is
implementation-specific and not guaranteed by standard C++.
